Hosting

This website is served via GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc. (USA). When you visit the website, GitHub processes technically necessary data such as your IP address, the time of access and details of the browser used in server log files. This data may be transferred to the USA. We have no access to these log files. For more information, see the GitHub privacy statement.

Contact form

If you write to us using the contact form, the details you enter (name, email address, optionally phone number, and your message) are transmitted to the Formspree service of Formspree, Inc. (USA) and forwarded from there to us by email. We use these details solely to handle your enquiry. For more information, see the Formspree privacy policy.

If you do not want this transfer to take place, you can write to us directly by email instead.

If you contact us by email, we process your details in order to answer your enquiry and any follow-up questions.
